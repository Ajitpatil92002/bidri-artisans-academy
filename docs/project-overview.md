# Project Overview

## What we're building

**bidriart.com** is an ecommerce website that sells Bidri art: the handmade metal inlay craft from Bidar, Karnataka, made by inlaying silver into a blackened zinc-copper alloy.

Ecommerce is the main part. Stories about the craft and its artisans support it, giving buyers a reason to trust the products and see their value. Each story should lead buyers back to the products.

## Who it's for

- **Buyers:** people in India and abroad shopping for authentic handmade Bidri pieces, for themselves or as gifts.
- **Artisans / the business:** need a place to show their work and tell their story.
- **Admin:** manages products, stories and orders without touching code.

## Feature scope

### Shop (main)
- Product catalogue with categories
- Product detail pages
- Cart
- Checkout with **Razorpay** (UPI, cards, netbanking), starting in test mode
- Order confirmation and order records

### Story / content
- **Artisan profiles:** each artisan's story, linked to the products they made
- **Craft history blog:** articles on Bidri history, technique and process
- **Story on product pages:** each product shows who made it and how

### Admin panel
- Add and edit products, artisans and blog posts
- View and manage orders
